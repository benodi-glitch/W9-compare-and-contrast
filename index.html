Paragraph 1 · Introduce and describe Corporate job

Start with a hook and your thesis, then describe the main features of Subject A. End by moving toward Subject B.

Sentence map (10 sentences)
  1. Hook: why does choosing a workplace matter?
  2. Introduce the two jobs you will compare.
  3. Thesis: your preference and main reason.
  4. Topic sentence: what makes Subject A distinctive?
  5. Feature 1 (for example, security)
  6. An example or result of feature 1.
  7. Feature 2 (for example, hierarchy or slow decisions)
  8. An example or result of feature 2.
  9. Feature 3, with a relative clause (a company that… / an employee who…)
  10. Bridge sentence: turn to Subject B.
Although…While…Unlike…a company that…an employee who…For example,As a result,
2

Paragraph 2 · Describe Startup job and contrast it

Show how Subject B is different. Use contrast words so the reader can see the comparison.

Sentence map (10 sentences)
  1. Transition and topic sentence for Subject B.
  2. Feature 1, contrasted with A (for example, pace)
  3. An example of feature 1.
  4. Feature 2 (for example, flexibility or multiple roles)
  5. An example or result of feature 2.
  6. A direct contrast with A using whereas or while.
  7. Feature 3 (for example, risk)
  8. The consequence of that risk.
  9. Feature 4, with a relative clause (a culture that… / a team whose…)
  10. Who would suit this job best?
In contrast,On the other hand,Whereas…However,Compared with……more … than…Unlike…
3

Paragraph 3 · Similarities and conclusion

Show what both jobs share, then finish with your preference in new words.

Sentence map (10 sentences)
  1. Transition: “Despite these differences…”
  2. Similarity 1 (for example, hard work)
  3. An example from each job.
  4. Similarity 2 (for example, teamwork)
  5. An example.
  6. Similarity 3 (for example, room to grow)
  7. An example.
  8. Similarity 4 (for example, deadlines)
  9. Restate your thesis in different words.
  10. Persuasive closing: advice, a prediction, or a question for the reader.
Similarly,Likewise,Both… and…Just as… so…Neither… nor…In the end,Overall,
